HCI principles applied

Visibility of system status

A 3-step progress bar, a ticket-hold timer that warns in the last two minutes and a live order total tell users where they are and what is happening.

Error prevention & recovery

Fees are shown before payment, ticket limits are explained and a failed payment keeps the order and offers clear next steps.

Consistency & standards

One colour accent, one card pattern, one button hierarchy and the same header on every page.

Recognition over recall

Active filters show as removable pills, event details repeat in the order summary and saved events are one tap away.

User control & freedom

Back links on every step, guest checkout, free cancellation and editable account settings.

Feedback

Every action answers back: inline field errors on blur, toasts after saving or cancelling, loading states on buttons and a live seat count.

Flexibility & efficiency

Press / to search from anywhere, filters live in the URL so Back works, logged-in details pre-fill checkout and saved cards are remembered.

Help & documentation

A searchable FAQ, contextual links from error pages straight to the right answer and this guided walkthrough of every flow.

Accessibility

Visible focus states, labelled fields, errors announced to screen readers, keyboard-operable seats, tabs and charts, a skip link and reduced-motion support.
