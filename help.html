Frequently asked

How do I get my ticket after booking?

Your e-ticket is emailed instantly and saved under My tickets. Show the QR code at the entrance, even offline.

Can I cancel and get a refund?

Yes. Cancel free up to 48 hours before the event from My tickets. Refunds reach your original payment method in 5 to 7 working days.

My payment failed. Was I charged?

No. If a payment fails, nothing is charged and your tickets stay held for the remaining hold time so you can retry or switch to UPI or net banking.

How long are tickets held while I pay?

Ten minutes from when you open the ticket page. The timer is always visible. If it runs out, your choices are kept and you can start a new hold.

Can I choose my seat?

For Premium seated tickets, yes. After adding them, pick your seats on the map. Taken seats are greyed out.

Can I transfer tickets to a friend?

Open the ticket in My tickets and choose Share. Your friend receives their own QR code.

I didn’t get the confirmation email.

Check your spam folder first. Your ticket is always available in My tickets, and you can download it from there.

How do I list my own event as an organizer?

Go to For organizers, create an event in four steps and publish it. It appears on Events straight away and you can track sales on your dashboard.
